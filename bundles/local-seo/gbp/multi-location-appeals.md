Google's 5 November 2026 Small Business Bulletin describes an appeals tool that accepts supporting files and an explanation before submission. The current Help page documents that sequence for profile suspensions both within and outside the UK and European Economic Area (EEA). Update training to assemble evidence before opening the tool. Some language variants still describe an older linked form. Follow the sequence the tool shows. [Google source notes](/references/google-gbp-appeals.md)

# Multi-location audit

* Keep a location register with each Business Profile ID, the Google account or group that can access the profile, and the person responsible for an appeal.
* For each restricted profile, record the decision type, reason, policy link, eligibility shown in the tool, and the branch's evidence status. Check that the name and address on any supporting documents match the profile as Google directs.
* Keep access to supporting documents restricted. Use the register to track where evidence is held and who can submit it.

# Training and escalation

1. Sign in to the Google account linked to the affected profile. Review the decision and resolve any actual guideline issue before submitting. Where a Google Account is restricted, appeal the account restriction first, then appeal the suspended profiles.
2. Prepare the relevant registration, licence, tax certificate or utility records and a concise factual explanation. Add the files and explanation in the appeals tool before confirming the submission.
3. For bulk reinstatement, follow the live prompt when offered. Google documents a prompt for accounts managing more than 10 profiles that asks whether there are at least 10 suspended locations. Attach a spreadsheet with each suspended location's Business Profile ID and the supporting evidence. If the option is missing, use the route Google shows for the affected profile or consult Google support.
4. Use the appeals tool for suspensions in all regions. For rejected business-information edits and media restrictions, Google directs UK and EEA businesses to the appeals tool and other businesses to Business Profile support. Review-removal appeals use the reviews management tool.
5. Log the submission centrally and check its status in the appeals tool. For bulk reinstatement, Google documents a status update and a decision email for the selected profile only. Track the other locations in your register. Google says a decision can take up to five business days. Avoid duplicate appeals for the same issue while it is pending and avoid creating a replacement profile for the same business.

# Client recommendation

Have head office coordinate the appeal and have each affected branch supply its own matching evidence. Agree who will submit and track the case before anyone presses Submit. Check the route for each market in a cross-border network.
