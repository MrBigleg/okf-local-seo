# Google Business Profile 2026: Universal Master Playbook

This is a RIM learning adaptation of Google's official 2026 guidance for Google Search and Maps. Google's playbook reports **over 2 billion direct connections per month in 2024** (calls, directions, messages, bookings, and reviews) for more than **19 million American businesses** [1, PDF p. 3].

Use the linked Google PDF and current Help Center policies as the source of official guidance. RIM examples and cowork practices explain how to apply it; they are not Google requirements. Features depend on business eligibility, category, region, and the controls currently shown in the profile.

---

## 1. Key Performance Evidence & Benchmarks

The PDF reports Google data and third-party studies. These benchmarks and individual case studies are context, not guaranteed improvements or proof that one change caused a result:

* **Complete Profile Multiplier**: Businesses with complete profiles receive **7x more clicks** than those with incomplete profiles [1].
* **Active Profile Attention**: Profiles that are regularly updated receive **5x more views** [1].
* **Purchase Intent**: **29% of consumers** are more likely to consider purchasing from a business with a detailed, complete profile [1].
* **Opening Hours Value**: The PDF cites Google data and Uberall's 2025 report for **96%** being more likely to visit a business displaying hours and **91%** searching online before a local visit [1, PDF p. 6].
* **Visual Engagement**: The PDF reports **42% more direction requests** and **35% more website clicks** for businesses with photos, plus a **90%** photo-related visit preference. These are reported comparisons, not a forecast for your business [1, PDF p. 10].
* **Review Response Impact**: The PDF cites SOCi's 2025 Consumer Behavior Index for **91%** using reviews to evaluate businesses and **65%** being more likely to choose a business that replies [1, PDF p. 15].
* **Messaging Preference**: The PDF cites Partoo's 2024 study and Twilio for **67%** preferring business messaging to calls/email and **60%** preferring WhatsApp as a store chat channel [1, PDF p. 13].
* **Promotion Velocity**: **50% of consumers** actively seek promotions or discounts when searching for local businesses online [1].

## 2. Google's 13-Point Optimization Framework

Google's PDF lists 13 areas of profile information and customer interaction [1, PDF p. 2]. RIM uses them as a learning framework:

### 1. Business Information & Identity
* **Business Name**: Use the real-world name consistently used on your storefront, website, stationery, and by customers [2]. It need not be the legal registration name. Do not append extra services, cities, or promotional phrases for search visibility; a service or city genuinely belonging to the established brand name is not automatically prohibited. Check the representation policy and business evidence before proposing a change.
* **Website Link**: Use a fast, mobile-optimized canonical URL.

### 2. Business Categories (1 Primary + up to 9 Secondary)
* **Primary Category**: Acts as Google's primary classification signal. Must represent your core business identity (e.g., "Italian Restaurant" or "Electrician").
* **Secondary Categories**: Up to 9 additional categories detailing secondary services or products.
* **Rule**: Keep categories specific; do not add unrelated categories.

### 3. Business Description (750 Characters)
* A concise overview of your offerings, values, and distinct advantages.
* Follow GBP's description policy: explain the business and avoid irrelevant content, links, or a focus on prices and promotions [2].
* **RIM writing practice**: Use confirmed business facts and write for people. Google Search Central's helpful-content guidance concerns website/search content; RIM adapts its principles for drafting rather than treating it as a separate GBP policy [3].

### 4. Operating Hours & Holiday Hours
* Specify accurate daily opening and closing times.
* Configure **Special & Holiday Hours** in advance for festive periods, public holidays, or temporary closures.
* Leverage specialized sub-hours where relevant: "Drive-through", "Delivery", "Takeout", "Happy hour", or "Online service hours".
* Check industry-specific exceptions: hotels' check-in/out details differ from ordinary opening hours; see the [hotel guide](hotels.md).

### 5. Service Area Footprint
* Mandatory for Service-Area Businesses (SABs with no public storefront) and Hybrid businesses (physical shop + delivery/field service).
* **Entry limit**: Maximum of **20 service areas** per profile [4].
* **Travel guidance**: Google advises an overall boundary of **about 2 hours of driving time** from the business base [4]. Review actual operating areas; this is approximate guidance, not a hard circular radius or an automatic suspension threshold.

### 6. Business Attributes
* Highlights specific features that match high-intent search filters: "Wheelchair accessible", "Free Wi-Fi", "Outdoor seating", "Identifies as women-owned", etc.
* Keep updated as physical amenities change.

### 7. Offerings Catalog (Services, Menus, Inventory)
* 1 out of 3 customers inspect offerings prior to visiting [1].
* Structure individual services or dishes with item titles, 2-line descriptions, and clear pricing.

### 8. Photos & Videos
* High-resolution, authentic imagery showcasing exterior signage, interior atmosphere, team members, and real products.
* **RIM practice**: Prefer authentic business media that accurately represents the place, people, and offerings. Do not promise a ranking reward for a particular photo style.

### 9. Google Posts (Cadence & Content)
* Maintain an active posting cadence of **at least once per week** [1].
* Utilize the 3 primary post types: **Updates**, **Offers** (with coupon codes/dates), and **Events**.
* Posts share timely information in Search and Maps. Do not infer an algorithmic freshness or ranking boost from posting frequency.
* Apply current category restrictions: hotels cannot create offer posts or posts mentioning/linking promotions, deals, special offers, or discounts [8].

### 10. Social Media Profile Integration
* Link official social profiles directly: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok.
* Case study evidence (Painting with a Twist using SOCi): The PDF reports **+9% GBP impressions**, **+10% web clicks and calls**, and **+14% Google-sourced users** after adding social links. These are that business's reported results, not expected course outcomes [1, PDF p. 12].

### 11. Chat & Direct Messaging
* Connect direct communication via WhatsApp Click-to-Chat URL or SMS text messaging.
* Choose a channel the team can maintain; the messaging preference figures are reported in the PDF [1, PDF p. 13].

### 12. Bookings, Orders & Appointments
* Enable "Reserve with Google" (available in 88+ countries) or provide custom direct booking/ordering URLs [1].
* The PDF's **77%** expectation concerns booking services online, not a universal table-reservation benchmark [1, PDF p. 14]. A custom booking link and a provider-integrated booking button are different features.

### 13. Review Generation & Reputation Response
* Google recommends replying to **both positive and negative reviews** [1, PDF p. 15]. **RIM coaching target**: Aim for replies within 48 to 72 hours when staffing permits; Google does not prescribe this response SLA.
* Deploy on-premise Google Review QR codes (on receipts, tables, menus, storefront windows) generated directly from your GBP short link.
* **Research distinction**: The **18-day** review-recency observation comes from a Sterling Sky case study [7], not Google's playbook or a universal ranking deadline. Seek honest customer feedback consistently; do not manufacture, gate, or incentivize reviews.

## 3. Account Governance, Security & Safeguards

Google's playbook includes access continuity and troubleshooting guidance [1]. RIM adds practical review habits below:

### Multiple Trusted Managers & Access Continuity
* **Vulnerability**: Businesses frequently lose access when an employee, agency, or former owner departs with primary credentials.
* **Google recommendation**: Add multiple trusted managers and check the access list regularly [1, PDF p. 20]. The PDF does not mandate a minimum headcount.
* **RIM resilience choice**: Where appropriate, nominate at least two trusted people with separate accounts to reduce reliance on one person. This is an optional operating practice, not a Google eligibility rule.
* **Ownership Hierarchy**: One *Primary Owner* can coexist with other *Owners* and *Managers* [5]. **RIM practice**: Review access quarterly and whenever staff or agencies change; retain only people who need it.

### Verification Standards
* Google determines the verification methods available to a business; follow the options shown and current verification guidance [6].
* **Video Verification Preparation**: If video is offered, record the required continuous clip and follow Google's instructions for your business type [6]. Typical evidence includes:
  1. Permanent exterior signage and street view showing the surrounding area/landmarks.
  2. Interior workspace, tools, and commercial equipment.
  3. Proof of management authority (unlocking the entrance, operating POS/cash register, or showing business registration docs).
* Google says verification review can take **up to 5 business days**; this is guidance, not a guaranteed completion time [1, PDF p. 17; 6].

### Official Google Support & Escalation Matrix
When profile disruptions occur, follow Google's prescribed escalation pathways [1]:

| Issue | Google Prescribed Action | Resolution Pathway |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lost Profile Access** | Request ownership | Submit ownership transfer request via `business.google.com/add` [1]. |
| **Suspended / Disabled Profile** | File appeal | Use official Google Business Profile Appeals Tool [1]. |
| **Duplicate Listing** | Support ticket | Submit merge/removal ticket via Google Support [1]. |
| **Hacked Profile / Unauthorized Edit** | Security ticket | File an emergency account compromise ticket [1]. |
| **Fake Reviews / Extortion** | Flag & report | Flag review against review content policy, then report extortion to Google Support [1]. |

## 4. Agent Cowork Operational Guidelines

When an AI agent (A2A or MCP) collaborates on a Google Business Profile under this playbook:

1. **Read-Only Verification First**: Query live profile facts using `rim_get_business_facts` before making recommendations.
2. **Check Name Evidence**: Reject extra search terms added to the name; preserve genuine established branding under Google's representation policy [2].
3. **Review Service Areas**: Check the 20-entry limit and approximate two-hour travel guidance against actual operations; request evidence for uncertain cases [4].
4. **Human Staging Checkpoint**: Always stage updates through `rim_stage_action_for_approval` or `rim_save_content_draft`. Never attempt direct autonomous writes to live listings.
5. **RIM Writing Practice**: Use confirmed facts, a clear purpose, and the applicable GBP content policy. The website-focused *Who, How, Why* questions are a useful RIM drafting aid, not a Google requirement for every GBP reply [3].
6. **Evidence Before Outcomes**: Save the action, source, before/after proof, and comparable measurement windows. Report observed change, including unchanged or worse results; do not attribute every change to the course.
