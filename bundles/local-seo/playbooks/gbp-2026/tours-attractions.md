# Google Business Profile 2026: Tours, Activities & Attractions

This RIM guide adapts Google's 2026 tours and attractions playbook into practical tasks. Read the original PDF and current ticket policies alongside it. Check the controls and provider details actually available to the business; do not assume every profile or partner exposes the same features.

---

## 1. Key Performance Evidence & Experience Benchmarks

The PDF's benchmarks are reported research, not guaranteed outcomes for an attraction or proof that completing a lesson caused a change.

* **Surging Experience Demand**: Searches for **"unique things to do near me" have grown globally by over 100% year-over-year** [1].
* **Ticket Information**: Ticket and activity details help travelers research their options; booking links take customers to the provider's website to buy or book [2]. Do not describe every ticket as an in-Google checkout.
* **Visual Inspiration**: The PDF repeats profile-photo benchmarks of **42% more direction requests**, **35% more website clicks**, and **90%** being more likely to visit a business with photos. They do not isolate the effect of video tours or promise an attraction-specific lift [1, PDF p. 11].

## 2. Tours & Attractions Optimization Pillars

### A. Activity & Ticket Integration Pathways
Google's PDF describes two ways to supply ticket or activity details [1, PDF p. 10]. Eligibility and price policies still apply [2, 3]:

#### Option 1: Google Ticket Editor (Direct & Free)
* **Accessibility**: The PDF describes the Google Ticket Editor as free. Open your profile's Tickets or Activities controls and follow the fields actually shown.
* **Setup Estimate**: The PDF estimates under five minutes for a first ticket; this is not an acceptance deadline or guarantee.
* **Required Fields**: Current help requires **Ticket or Activity name** and **Booking URL** [2]. Record any other fields offered by the live editor; do not invent mandatory adult/child, VIP, fast-track, or cancellation-policy controls.
* **Merchant Features**: The PDF mentions access to merchant features such as insights; verify the metrics actually available before assigning a measurement task.

#### Option 2: Connected Ticketing Partners
* **Existing Connection Check**: Ask the business about its current provider and consult Google's current connectivity information. Provider support and costs vary; a paid connection is optional for the Academy.
* **Price Maintenance**: An existing integration can reduce repeated manual updates, but does not guarantee price parity or instantaneous sync. Check the displayed price against the bookable price and follow the Things to do price policies [3].
* **Display Precedence**: Current help says connectivity-partner admission details appear ahead of manually supplied information [2]. Investigate the supplying partner if a manual change is not reflected.
* **Feature Availability**: The PDF describes earlier access to some features and ads via partners. Confirm the actual integration before promising access; starting paid ads is not part of the free path.

### B. Experience Attribute Optimization
**RIM planning examples**: Confirm relevant visitor information and use attributes actually available in the profile. Put remaining details on the official booking page; these examples are not a universal list of Google toggles:
* **Booking Requirements**: "Reservations required", "Walk-ins welcome", "Advance booking recommended".
* **Audience Suitability**: "Good for kids", "Pet-friendly tour", "Wheelchair accessible paths", "Rain or shine".
* **Language Support**: Guided tours offered in English, Thai, Spanish, Mandarin, etc.

### C. Capturing "Things to Do" Demand via Google Posts
Google's PDF recommends weekly posts [1]. Use accurate updates suited to the business:
1. **Seasonal Schedules & Slots**: Announce opening of holiday slots, sunset cruises, or seasonal exhibitions.
2. **Special Offers**: Early-bird discounts, family bundle passes, or resident rates (50% of consumers seek discounts online).
3. **Event Highlights**: Guest speakers, live performance schedules, and weekend workshops.

## 3. Agent Cowork Execution Protocol for Tours & Attractions

When an AI agent audits or collaborates with a tour operator or attraction:

1. **Ticketing Module Check (`rim_get_business_facts`)**:
   * Inspect the actual Tickets or Activities module, names, booking links, and any prices shown.
   * Ask whether details come from an existing partner or the manual editor; a provider name alone does not prove an active Google connection.
2. **Price Parity Audit**:
   * Check the same ticket/activity, date, participant conditions, currency, and included fees on Google and the linked booking page. Record the source and time of the comparison [3].
3. **Action Staging (`rim_stage_action_for_approval`)**:
   * Stage confirmed missing information only in supported fields; send other useful details to an owner-reviewed website or content draft.
   * Stage weekly event/offer Google Posts for upcoming weekend tours.
4. **Visual Content Recommendations**:
   * Flag listings lacking dynamic video clips or high-resolution participant action photos.
