Google's Business Profile Help page on representing a business, focusing on the Name guideline. The same page is the primary source for [GBP categories & description rules](/references/google-gbp-categories-description.md) and is cited in [Google Business Profile](/gbp/google-business-profile.md).

# What the source establishes

* A Business Profile name should reflect how the business is represented in the real world and be in a single consistent form.
* **Not permitted:** repeating the same business name in multiple scripts or languages — Google's guidelines list this as "Repeated Bilingual Names / Script Transliterations".
* **Examples:** a single-script name (`Kafiex`, `Burger King`) is acceptable; the dual-script variant (`Kafiex / カフィエクス`, `Burger King バーガーキング`) is explicitly not allowed.
* **Key change:** physical storefront signage showing the dual-script name is no longer an accepted justification — the prohibition is stated as effective retroactively. Previously, business owners could justify a dual-script name with storefront photos.

# Why it matters

* Directly affects businesses in multilingual and multi-script markets — Thailand (Thai/English), Japan (Kanji/Kana), the Middle East (Arabic), Europe, and any area using more than one script.
* For business owners currently using a repeated bilingual name, the compliance path is to choose one consistent name form; continued dual-script names risk Google editing the name or taking enforcement action.
* It is a content-guideline change, not a documented ranking factor, and does not imply any ranking effect.
