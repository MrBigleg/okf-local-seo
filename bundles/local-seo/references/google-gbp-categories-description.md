Google's Business Profile Help guidelines page covering categories and business descriptions. Used as the primary source in [GBP Business Description](/gbp/business-description.md) and [Industry Vertical Detection](/local-seo/industry-vertical-detection.md).

# What the source establishes

* Categories: use as few as possible to describe the core business, prefer specific categories over broad ones (Google automatically includes broader related categories), and never select categories solely as keywords or to describe unrelated services/nearby businesses.
* Description: must give useful, accurate information on products/services and business history; must not emphasise promotions or prices, include links, or contain low-quality/gimmicky/unrelated content.
