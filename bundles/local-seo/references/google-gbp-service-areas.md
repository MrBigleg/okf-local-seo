Google's Business Profile Help page on service-area configuration. Used as the primary source in [GBP Verification](/gbp/verification.md) and [Business Type Detection](/local-seo/business-type-detection.md).

# What the source establishes

* Service-area businesses (visit/deliver to customers, no storefront — cleaners, plumbers) must remove their address; hybrid businesses (storefront plus delivery/visits, e.g. a restaurant with delivery) keep the address and add service areas.
* Up to 20 service areas, each defined by city, postal code, or similar geographic designation — never as a radius — and Google recommends staying within roughly a two-hour drive of the business's base location.
