Google's help-centre documentation of Business Profile verification. Primary source for [GBP verification](/gbp/verification.md).

# What the source establishes

* Five methods are described: video recording, phone or text, email, live video call and postcard. Separately, Google says it sometimes verifies a profile instantly, for example when the website is verified in Google Search Console or through bulk verification. The page states no count.
* "Verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can't be changed"; the options offered depend on business type, public info, region or business hours.
* Post-submission review takes **up to 5 business days**; verification codes expire after 30 days; most postcards arrive within 14 days.

# Clarification carried into the bundle

Earlier versions of this bundle said "six methods". The help page gives no count and presents Search Console as a case of instant verification, not a selectable method, so the bundle no longer states a number.
