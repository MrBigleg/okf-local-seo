Google's Keyword post of 27 March 2024. Cited in [Google Maps lists](/maps/google-maps-lists.md).

# What the source establishes

* Google Maps shows curated lists of recommendations "from top sites and the Google Maps community" for select cities in the US and Canada.
* A user reaches them by searching for a city in the Maps app and swiping up.
* Three community lists are named: **Trending**, updated weekly with places that have recently seen a spike in popularity on Maps; **Top**, places the Maps community has consistently loved; and **Gems**, places that might be a neighbourhood's best-kept secret.
* Publisher lists sit alongside them. The post names The Infatuation, Lonely Planet, The New York Times (36 Hours) and OpenTable.
* The same post covers user-made lists and AI-generated place summaries.

# What it does not establish

The post does not say how places are selected, ordered or removed, what evidence a list entry shows, or which cities and countries have lists. Lists observed outside the US and Canada since then (for example the UK and Thailand) are covered by later observation, not by this source.
