Google's Maps/Business Profile contribution policy on review manipulation. Used as the primary source in [Review Gating](/gbp/review-gating.md), [Reviews on GBP](/gbp/reviews.md), and [GBP Spam & Fake Listings](/gbp/spam-and-fake-listings.md).

# What the source establishes

* Merchants must not offer incentives (payment, discounts, free goods/services) in exchange for a review, and must not selectively solicit only positive reviews.
* Merchants must not require or pressure customers to rate or review while on the premises.
* Soliciting or encouraging genuine reviews is permitted, provided the request does not shape the content or target only favourable outcomes.
