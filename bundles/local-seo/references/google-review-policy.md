Google's Maps and Business Profile contribution policy on review manipulation and merchant advertising. Used as the primary source in [Review Gating](/gbp/review-gating.md), [Reviews on GBP](/gbp/reviews.md), and [GBP Spam & Fake Listings](/gbp/spam-and-fake-listings.md).

# Review manipulation

* Merchants must not offer incentives (payment, discounts, free goods/services) in exchange for a review, and must not selectively solicit only positive reviews.
* Merchants must not require or pressure customers to rate or review while on the premises.
* Soliciting or encouraging genuine reviews is permitted, provided the request does not shape the content or target only favourable outcomes.

# Merchant advertising and solicitation

Google's current policy explicitly bars promotional or commercial listing content containing limited-time offers, prices or direct calls to action. It separately permits promotional Offers and Updates in Posts, subject to the rules on contact information, unrelated destinations and the [dedicated Posts policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077). See [Google Posts](/gbp/google-posts.md) for the publishing checklist.

The [earlier Google wording captured by Hiroko Imai](https://images.seroundtable.com/old-policy-jvU71CAK.jpg) gave a general ban on advertising or solicitation, with examples covering promotional or commercial content and contact details or links in reviews. It had no merchant-specific listing or Posts rules in this section. The revised wording was publicly reported with before-and-after screenshots by 6 October 2026 and confirmed in Google's English and British English policy on 28 November 2026.[^seroundtable-maps-advertising-policy-before-and-after] Google gives no effective date or geographic limitation in this section. Its [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) already restricted promotional business descriptions.
