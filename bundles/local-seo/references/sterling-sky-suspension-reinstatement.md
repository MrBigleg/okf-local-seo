# The seven steps as presented

1. **Confirm the suspension type.** A "soft" suspension means the profile still appears on Google but can no longer be managed. A "hard" suspension means it is gone from the map, and is the urgent case. Search Google Maps for the business name to see which applies. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
2. **Audit the profile for red flags.** The presenters name a virtual office, many edits in a short period, an address that does not match the business registration, and duplicate listings for the same business. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
3. **Check owners and managers.** They say that if any user on the profile has had their Google account suspended, the listing goes down with it, and advise removing restricted accounts before appealing. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
4. **Collect proof the business is real.** Utility bills, tax documents, business licences, and photos or videos of the physical location. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
5. **Clean the profile.** No keyword stuffing, no redirects, no service areas overlapping another of the business's listings. Use the real business name and a domain-based email for ownership. The video does not number this step aloud. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
6. **File the appeal with all evidence at once.** They call the appeal tool the only route to reinstatement and say a partial appeal can delay the outcome. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
7. **Avoid a repeat.** Make edits slowly, never use a virtual address, and be careful about how many users are added. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]

They also say not to create a new profile while suspended. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]

# Limits

* The video's title and closing lines speak of reinstatement in "days" or "weeks". No case, timeline or success rate is given, and this page makes no claim about how long reinstatement takes. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
* The causes listed in steps 2, 3 and 7 are the agency's experience of what triggers suspensions. The video cites no Google policy for them. [^notebooklm-source-9c796141]
* Before acting, check Google's own current guidance on suspensions and appeals; this page has not compared the seven steps against it.
