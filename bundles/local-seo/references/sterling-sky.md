Sterling Sky (founded by Joy Hawkins) publishes case-study research on local ranking. We cite it for the **18-day rule** — Sterling Sky's observation that rankings can "fall off a cliff" if a business stops receiving reviews "for even three weeks". It is a community-recognised pattern, **not** confirmed Google policy. Used in [reviews & reputation](/local-seo/reviews-reputation.md).
